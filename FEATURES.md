# Still: feature map

Everything Still has today, read from the code. ✅ shipped in 2.10.1 (`main`) · 🚧 in progress (branch named) · 🧪 experimental.
Paths: `still/` = Windows host (WPF, WebView2), `still-ui/src/` = the React interface both hosts load, `mac/Sources/Still/` = Mac host (AppKit, WebKit).

**Rule:** any commit that adds, changes or removes a feature updates this file in the same commit.

## Browser and tabs

- ✅ **Tabs**: open, close, pin, duplicate, mute, reorder, sleep; saved in `state.json` (`Models.cs` `SavedState`, written via `.tmp` then `.bak` swap).
- ✅ **Restore tabs**: reopens last session's tabs on start, every normal window with its own tabs (each tab saved with its `Window`); windows closed one after another within 15 seconds of the last all come back; never private tabs or a temporary window's; Preferences `RestoreTabs` (`SavedTabs`, `RestoreOtherWindows`; Mac `saveAll`, `restoreOtherWindows`, built but not run; tests/desktop/session.py).
- ✅ **Reopen closed tab**: Ctrl+Shift+T, plus a recently closed list (`recent`/`recentPick` in `MainWindow.Shell.cs`).
- ✅ **Recent-tab switcher**: Ctrl+Tab flips to the last tab; holding Ctrl shows an MRU list (`MainWindow.Tabs.cs` `SwitchRecent`).
- ✅ **Cycle tabs**: Ctrl+Alt+Left/Right moves through tabs in strip order (`CycleTabs`).
- ✅ **Find a tab**: Ctrl+K sheet filters open tabs (`MainWindow.Panels.cs`).
- ✅ **Tab groups**: named, colored, collapsible; members stay adjacent in the strip (`MainWindow.Tabs.cs`, `TabGroup`).
- ✅ **A group in two places**: a group whose tabs aren't side by side in the strip (a chat's page opened inside it, or chat and pinned tabs left out of the list) shows each part with its own key. Two parts with the group's id made the strip lose one, and closing a tab left copies of tabs on screen, more with each close (`App.tsx` `TabStrip`; tests/desktop/tabclose.py).
- ✅ **Split view**: two tabs side by side in one window, resizable, swappable; one pair per window (`StartSplit`). Swap sides, 50/50 and Separate tabs are in a split tab's right-click menu.
- ✅ **Tab layout**: Classic (tabs on top) or Modern (resizable left sidebar); Preferences `TabLayout`, `SidebarWidth`.
- ✅ **Multiple windows**: one process, shared history/bookmarks/settings; each window owns its tabs (`MainWindow.Windows.cs`).
- ✅ **Temporary sign-in windows**: windows Still opens itself for a sign-in (Composio's Get a key page, an app's Composio sign-in) are temporary: never saved to the session, closed with the last normal window, and never given links from other apps or a companion's tabs; with only temporary or private windows left the saved session stays as it was (`temporary`, `Unsaved`, `Home`). While setup is showing, a link from another app or an AI app's sign-in page opens in one of these, in front of setup, never as a tab behind it (`OpenOverSetup`, Mac `openOutside`; tests/desktop/welcome.py).
- ✅ **Move tabs between windows**: drag out (setting "Drag tabs into their own window", off by default) or Move to; live page moves without reload.
- ✅ **Private window**: every tab InPrivate and unsaved; Ctrl+Shift+N (`OpenIncognito`). Each incognito window has its own private engine profile on Windows too (`ownPrivateProfile`), so two never share a sign-in; private tabs in a normal window share one.
- ✅ **Incognito AI chats**: a chat in a private window keeps nothing. It's never in chats.json, history, Resume or another window (`privateChats`, `Ephemeral`). Its AI app runs in `ai/incognito/<window>` with its attachments and failure logs; closing the window stops its runs and deletes the folder, and the next start sweeps what a crash left. Its pages, web_search and read_url use the private engine profile (the InPrivate profile on Windows, the window's own private website data on the Mac), never either persistent profile, and it uses only the tabs it opened. Connected apps are off there: the plugin tools aren't offered (`--no-plugins`), a call is refused, and the chat says so. remember, update_knowledge, forget, save_file and offer_routine are refused, no site rule is saved and drafts stay in memory. The chat says the AI's provider and the sites it visits still get what's sent, and the provider's app may keep its own record (`tests/desktop/incognito.py`).
- ✅ **Incognito runs ask the AI app not to save**: from an incognito chat, Claude Code runs with `--no-session-persistence` and ChatGPT with `exec --ephemeral` (both documented in their `--help`), so each turn starts a new session that carries the chat so far, and the chat says the app is told not to save it. Grok has no such option, so its chat still says it may keep its own record.
- ✅ **Address bar**: search or URL; local history/bookmark suggestions, learned typed→picked ranking (`suggestions.ts`, `AddressPick`); never fetches icons or sends keystrokes.
- ✅ **still:// pages**: still://chats (also still://chat-history), new, library, companions, skills, settings, history, bookmarks, downloads, passwords, profiles, import, extensions, about and help open Still's own pages; any case, spaces read as dashes; typing still:// suggests them; an unknown one says so instead of searching (`STILL_PAGES`, `go` in App.tsx; tests/desktop/stillpages.py). Typed in the address bar only: bookmarks and links to still:// don't open them.
- ✅ **Search engine**: Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo (Preferences `SearchEngine`).
- ✅ **New tab page**: the companion home (chats, ideas) or "sites" (speed dial, suggested sites, open tabs); Preferences `NewTab`.
- ✅ **Try one of these**: up to three concrete one-click tasks under the home's message box, each with a one-line why, strongest first: a project the companion remembers ("From Maya's memory"), a connected app it works in ("Gmail is connected"), a real open tab (never a private, loading, chat or non-web one), then its role's tasks that match the work the user picked in setup, then its role's own. They follow the context as it changes; with nothing to go on there are no cards at all (`homeIdeas` in suggestions.ts; still-ui/tests/suggestions.test.ts, tests/desktop/ideas.py)
- ✅ **Bookmarks**: Ctrl+D, bookmarks bar, edit/move/remove, import/export HTML (`MainWindow.Panels.cs`).
- ✅ **History**: searchable panel, remove entries, clear all (`MainWindow.Panels.cs`).
- ✅ **Downloads**: card on start, progress ring, open/show/cancel, choose folder (`MainWindow.xaml.cs`, Preferences `DownloadFolder`). 🚧 (security/fixes) On the Mac a file a site sends as an attachment downloads even when it could be shown, as on Windows (`main.swift` `decidePolicyFor response`; `attachment.py`). On Windows a download a pop-up window starts goes to your folder and the downloads card too, and keeps going if the site closes the window (`MainWindow.Popups.cs`, `Download`).
- ✅ **Reader mode**: Ctrl+Shift+R, Mozilla Readability on a copy of the page (`MainWindow.Actions.cs`, `Assets/Readability.js`).
- 🚧 (security/fixes) **Report a bug**: the main menu's Report a bug, under About Still, opens GitHub's bug report form for Still (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.yml`) in a new tab (`App.tsx`).
- ✅ **Hide an element**: pick an element to hide; per-site rules re-applied on load (`PickHidden`, `SavedState.Hidden`).
- ✅ **Find in page, zoom, picture-in-picture**: toolbar and shortcuts (`find`, `zoom`, `pictureInPicture` in `MainWindow.Shell.cs`).
- ✅ **Full screen**: F11 covers the monitor incl. taskbar; video full screen fills the window instantly (`MainWindow.FullScreen.cs`).
- ✅ **Pop-ups**: sized `window.open` gets a small window titled with its site; unrequested pop-ups blocked, allow per site (`MainWindow.Popups.cs`). 🚧 (security/fixes) The Mac too: the bar offers pop-ups no person started, and Allow opens them (`main.swift` `createWebViewWith`, `Tools.swift` `popupBlocked`).
- ✅ **App links**: discord:, mailto: etc. ask in the permission bar, remember Allow per site+app, refuse abusable schemes (`MainWindow.AppLinks.cs`).
- ✅ **Site permissions**: camera/mic/location etc. asked in Still's own bar; denied after 90 s unanswered (`MainWindow.Permissions.cs`).
- ✅ **Permissions button**: in the toolbar where split view's button was: Allow, Ask or Block per site for camera, microphone, location, notifications, clipboard, pop-ups, automatic downloads, autoplay and sound, screen sharing, MIDI and USB; applied at once and kept (a private window's only until it closes); Reset; a link to all site settings; a dot while the page has the camera, mic or location on (`SitePermissions.tsx`, `MainWindow.Permissions.cs`, Mac `Tools.swift`).
- ✅ **Page dialogs**: alert/confirm/prompt shown inside Still, not as Windows dialogs (`dialogAnswer`).
- 🚧 (security/fixes) **Upload card**: a site's file picker opens a card of recent matching files, copied image first (as a JPEG for a site that only takes those); picker intercepted via CDP (`MainWindow.FilePaste.cs`, `UploadCard.tsx`).
- 🚧 (security/fixes) **Draft keeper**: text typed into a box survives a surprise reload; a chip offers it back for 6 h (`MainWindow.Drafts.cs`). Sending the form forgets it, boxes named for a secret and text that looks like a key or token are never kept, and Save drafts off stops it for new tabs.
- ✅ **Audio follows the default output**: on a Windows output switch, playing media reopens on the new device; 1.5 s poll (`MainWindow.Audio.cs`).
- ✅ **Tabs keep their state**: switching hides a tab's page, never reloads or unloads it, so scroll, typed text, sound and page state stay (`SelectTab`, Mac `select`); `tests/desktop/tabkeep.py`.
- ✅ **Memory saver**: background tabs sleep after 1 min (state kept); only under real memory pressure (Windows 90% RAM in use, a macOS memory-pressure warning) does an untouched one unload, and it reloads where it was scrolled to; media, downloads, pinned and typed-in tabs never unload (`MainWindow.Memory.cs`, Mac `sleepIdleTabs`). 🚧 (security/fixes) The 3D companions' model decoder runs one worker and lets go as soon as nothing is loading (`rig.ts`).
- 🚧 (security/fixes) **Message box drafts**: every chat's box, the side chat's and the New Tab's keep what you typed, its mentions and its attached files as you type (half a second after the last change, and on leaving the box or Still). Still keeps them in its own files (`ai/drafts`, one per box), so they come back after a refresh, closing the tab or chat, a crash or a restart, quietly, with no notice, with the caret where it was. An edit of your latest message is kept the same way, per chat: its text, its caret and the edit box still open. Cancel keeps it for the next Edit, and Send keeps it until the chat shows the new message, so an edit Still refuses isn't lost; it goes once sent, or once another message is the latest. Each chat has its own; the main window's New Tab has one (another window's lasts while that window is open). A routine's What to do, setup's answer box and the answer box on a companion's question keep drafts too. The draft goes once Still takes the message; a send it can't take (over 20,000 characters) says why, keeps the draft and puts the text back. Attached files not sent stay 7 days; a draft not touched for 7 days goes. A private window keeps nothing on disk. Drafts the interface kept itself before move into the files on the first start. Each chat comes back where you'd scrolled (`drafts.tsx`, `Composer` `draft`, `MyMessage` `draft`, `useDraft`, Windows `SaveDraft`, Mac `saveDraft`, `aiReattach`).
- ✅ **Undo and redo in text boxes**: Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z (and Ctrl+Y on Windows; Cmd on the Mac, and Edit → Undo) in every Still text box except password and key fields, step by step: typing runs together until a pause or a new word, and select all and delete, cut, paste, drop, Discard, Send emptying the box and a draft coming back are a step each. A box with a draft keeps its last 50 steps when you switch chats or tabs, and with Save drafts on, through a restart (`drafts.tsx`, Mac `ShellView` for the menu).
- ✅ **Tab strip and bookmarks bar**: the selected tab fits inside the strip, the bookmarks bar has its own solid background; no overlap in either theme at any width or display scale (`App.css`); `tests/desktop/tabstrip.py`.
- ✅ **Error and crash pages**: one page per load failure ("you're offline" etc.); renderer crash shows in place, no auto-reload (`ErrorPages.cs`, `MainWindow.xaml.cs`).
- ✅ **Engine crash recovery**: if the engine's main process dies, Still saves tabs and restarts once (`App.xaml.cs`).
- ✅ **Themes**: Light, Dark, System (Preferences `Theme`).

## Blocking and privacy

- ✅ **Ad and tracker blocking**: built-in third-party domain list answered with 204; per-site off switch (`MainWindow.Actions.cs` `BlockedDomains`, `UnblockedHosts`).
- ✅ **Tracking prevention**: Basic, Balanced or Strict via WebView2 `PreferredTrackingPreventionLevel` (Preferences `Tracking`).
- ✅ **Cookies panel**: search and delete cookies per cookie or per site (`MainWindow.BrowserTools.cs`).
- ✅ **Form autofill**: on/off for the engine's general autofill; password autosave always off (own manager instead).
- ✅ **No telemetry**: no account, no analytics; the only background request is the GitHub release check.

## Profiles, import and export

- ✅ **Profiles**: separate logins/cookies/tabs; each opens as its own process (`ProfileCatalog.cs`, `MainWindow.Profiles.cs`).
- ✅ **Temp profile**: throwaway window with no cookies or history, deleted once closed (`App.xaml.cs`; MCP `profile: "temp"`).
- ✅ **Mac interface storage per profile**: on macOS 14 and later each profile's interface keeps its own storage (settings the interface holds); deleting a profile removes it (`Profiles.shellStore`).
- ✅ **Import from another browser (Windows)**: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera/GX, Firefox: bookmarks, history, passwords, cookies (sessions), autofill; reviewed before commit. A browser signed in to an account keeps that account's bookmarks and passwords in their own files (`AccountBookmarks`, `Login Data For Account`); both are read. When everything was imported before, the result says how many were already in Still (`ExternalProfileImport.cs`; tests/desktop/browserimport.py).
- ✅ **Import from another browser (Mac)**: Safari, Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, Vivaldi, Opera, Firefox: bookmarks, history and passwords, reviewed first, the signed-in account's bookmarks and passwords too, and the result says how many were already in Still. From Chromium browsers also cookies, so you stay signed in (one Keychain request covers passwords and cookies; if macOS doesn't share the key, the review says so with macOS's error number) (`BrowserLogins.chromium`, `finishImport`; tests/desktop/passwords.py). When macOS's privacy protection keeps Still out of a browser's folder, the panel says so and offers Choose <browser>'s folder (an Open panel already at that folder: one click on Open lets Still in) and Open Full Disk Access (`reviewImport`, `importBlocked`).
- ✅ **Close source and retry**: a running Chromium browser locks its cookies; with your OK Still closes it and reads again (`CloseSourceAndRetry`).
- ✅ **File import/export**: bookmarks HTML/JSON, passwords CSV, Still data JSON (tabs, bookmarks, history) (`MainWindow.Import.cs`).

## Passwords and autofill

- ✅ **Password manager (Windows)**: save, update, fill, delete, never-save list; vault encrypted with DPAPI (`Vault.cs`, `MainWindow.Passwords.cs`, `Assets/LoginObserver.js`).
- ✅ **Password manager (Mac)**: same features. 🚧 (security/fixes) Kept in the profile in a file encrypted with a key that needs this Mac's Secure Enclave (its file, `secrets-<this Mac>.key` beside the profiles, works only on this Mac; a Mac without an Enclave uses one made from the Mac's id and your user id), not the Keychain, so updates no longer bring Keychain prompts; Still reads the Keychain only when you import: Import > An older Still brings over the logins and keys an older Still kept there (macOS asks once for each), and a Chrome-family import reads that browser's key (`Vault.swift` `Sealed`, `DataTools.tsx`). API keys and the Composio key too.
- 🚧 (security/fixes) **Saved logins stay out of a page's scripts**: Still's login helper only ever starts before a page's own scripts, and a form that would send what's filled to another site gets nothing.
- 🚧 (security/fixes) **Key in the field**: login fields get a fill button for this exact site's saved logins once you touch the field, and a list the page has hidden or covered fills nothing; optional auto sign-in fills at your first click in a real, visible field, never as the page opens.
- 🚧 (security/fixes) **Strong passwords**: suggests one for new accounts and saves it immediately so it's never lost; over a login that's already saved it fills the form and asks with the Update bar once the form is sent.
- ✅ **Two-step sign-ins**: remembers the username from step one for the password step.
- ✅ **Insecure pages**: filling on non-https asks first; auto sign-in stays off there (localhost excepted).
- ✅ **OS check before reveal**: Windows Hello/PIN or Touch ID before show, copy, edit or export, at most once a minute (`OsAuth.cs`).
- ✅ **Weak password flags**: short, one or two character kinds, common, or repetitive (`MainWindow.Passwords.cs`).
- ✅ **CSV import/export**: Chrome/Edge/Firefox-compatible CSV; import can replace matching logins.
- ✅ **Never in private tabs**: no prompts, no saving, no filling there.
- ✅ **Kept from AIs**: AIs can't fill while driving a tab; fields holding saved usernames read as hidden; sign-in pages pause the AI for you.

## Memory

- ✅ **Per-companion memory**: `companions/<id>/memory.json`, local plain JSON, used only in that companion's chats (`CompanionMemory.cs`).
- ✅ **remember / recall / forget tools**: Still's own chat only; recall ranks by shared words, core facts, then newest.
- ✅ **Where a memory came from**: each memory keeps its chat and message, its source (said in a chat, guessed, added in Settings, told in setup), who it's about and the date. A newer version supersedes it (`replaces` or the same slot): the old one stays as history, never goes into a prompt, and the memory page lists it under Earlier versions. A correction replaces the closest one about the same person. The same thing said again freshens the old one instead of copying it. One memory is 300 characters at most (`Fact.V` 2; files from before 2.10 migrate on first read; tests/desktop/recall.py).
- ✅ **open_memory_source**: the companion can read the messages a memory or a past chat came from, to check what you actually said; a deleted chat leaves only the memory.
- ✅ **Forget for good**: forget by id takes every version; forget by words that match more than three memories forgets nothing and lists them to check with you. What's forgotten leaves a tombstone (hashes, never its text): it's refused if saved again, and left out of summaries, unless you say it again yourself (`forgotten.json`).
- ✅ **Recall that says when it doesn't know**: BM25 over memories and chat summaries, light stemming and a fixed alias list (mom/mother, job/work, browser/chrome…), with a floor, so an unrelated question finds nothing and the tool says not to guess. Profile items always come, in their own small budget; a chat's first turn gets what fits the message and the chat so far, about 1,500 characters.
- ✅ **Chat summaries**: a chat quiet for 10 minutes with at least two of your messages gets one summary on its AI's quickest model: goal, constraints, decisions, corrections, done, open and next step, each line linked to its messages. A run that didn't finish has nothing done (its lines read "Not finished"). Never incognito, off-the-record, delegated or routine chats, nor chats from before 2.10. What they remember has the Summarize chats switch (`ChatSummaries`) and lists each companion's summaries with Forget this chat (`summaries.json`). Replaces the old one-line past chats, which migrate as "from before 2.10, not checked".
- ✅ **Change notes**: an open chat's next turn hears, once and briefly, what changed outside it since its session started: memories added, updated or forgotten (by id only), instructions changed, knowledge rewritten or reset.
- ✅ **Knowledge files**: a hired role's working notes (`companions/<id>/knowledge/*.md`, up to 20). The prompt lists them (name, version, size, last update), not their text; `read_knowledge` reads one and `update_knowledge` needs the version it read, so a stale write is refused with the current text (12000 characters at most). The last 10 versions stay: the profile has Undo last change, and the memory page has Reset knowledge (back to the role's empty seeds). An incognito chat reads them, never writes (tests/desktop/memory.py, recall.py).
- ✅ **Never stored**: passwords, codes, keys, card/account numbers, form input the AI typed, anything from private tabs.
- ✅ **Secret filter by shape**: known key prefixes, JWTs, private keys, card numbers that pass Luhn, IBANs that pass mod-97, random-looking tokens and "password is …" with a value; "my password is saved in 1Password" is fine. Applies to memory, knowledge files and summary lines.
- ✅ **Memory files that survive a crash**: every write goes to a temp file, then replaces the old one, which stays as .bak. A broken file is kept as `memory.corrupt-<time>.json`, the backup loads, and Still says so once; a write that fails changes nothing and the companion is told it wasn't saved.
- ✅ **Relevant memory only**: a chat's first turn gets the facts that share a word with the message or are core (name, likes, people, and anything the user wrote in setup or on the memory page), and past chats only when related. A fact a companion worked out from what you did is saved as guessed: its prompt lists those apart as not confirmed, the memory page marks them Guessed, and editing one confirms it.
- ✅ **Memory page**: Settings → Companions → What they remember: view, add, edit, forget, clear per companion (`Memory.tsx`).
- ✅ **Memory controls**: per companion, Remember new things (off: nothing new is kept, what's there is still read), Forget this chat, Clear memory, Reset knowledge, and Reset everything (memory, summaries, knowledge and Setup; its chats start new sessions). Clear personalization resets Role, Personality and Reply length and forgets what Setup learned. Introduce yourself again sets Setup pending; its next chat says they've met (`memoryResetAll`, `personalizationClear`, `companionSetupAgain`).
- ✅ **Setup state**: a companion's Setup is pending until it has answered you once in a regular chat (never incognito, delegated or routine), then done with its date and the Setup skill's version. A hidden note tells it what Still knows: first use or met before, how many things you've told Still, crew or Make your own, and whether your message is its first task. A user-edited role with the old "When you join the team" section gets an offer to take it out (`Setup`, `Activation`, `instructionsJoin`; `Greet` migrates).

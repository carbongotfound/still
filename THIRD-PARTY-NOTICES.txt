ORIGINAL WORK
The companion mascots are Still's own: the 3D models and animations in
still-ui/public/companion (*.glb, fit.json), the portraits in still-ui/src/assets/companion,
the faces the app draws on them, and the Blender scripts that build and render them
(tools/mascot). So are the app icon (still/Assets/still.ico, still.png) and the favicon.
They contain no third-party models, textures, fonts or art, and they are covered by
Still's MIT license like the rest of the code.

Still ships no sound files and no AI models. It works through the AI service you
sign into yourself.

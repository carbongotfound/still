---
name: Split and merge
description: Splits a bigger job across the team and brings back one answer.
when: A request with parts for different teammates
---

## When
A request has two or more parts that different teammates own, like a prospect brief and an outreach draft.

## Inputs
- The request, and the team from list_companions.
- Requests, for anything related that's still open.

## Steps
1. Write each part as an assignment: what to do, the inputs, what to bring back, and a limit (length, sources, time).
2. When one part needs another's result, ask for the first, then pass its result into the second.
3. Read each result. Keep its sources and what the teammate wasn't sure of.
4. Merge into one answer in the order the user needs it, with who did what. Keep each deliverable whole: a teammate's drafts go in as they wrote them, each in its own ```draft block, never a summary of them. Shorten only notes about how the work went.
5. Note the request and anything left open in Requests.

## Check
- Every part of the request is answered or named as missing.
- Facts in the answer come with the teammate's source.
- Every draft the user asked for is in the answer itself, not only in a teammate's task.

## Deliverable
One answer, then a line per teammate on what they did, then sources and open points.

## If it goes wrong
- A part failed or was stopped: say exactly what's missing and offer to try it again.
- Two results disagree: show both and say which you'd trust and why.

## Limits
Assignments stay inside the request. Teammates follow the user's rules and yours, whichever is stricter, and every approval goes to the user.

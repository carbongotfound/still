---
name: Bug report
description: What happened, what should happen and how to repeat it.
when: Writing up a bug
---

1. Reproduce it yourself when you can, and say whether you could.
2. Write: a one-line title, what happened, what should happen, numbered steps, where (browser, page, account type) and how bad it is.
3. Follow the format in Your conventions when there is one.
4. Add it to Known bugs. Filing it in a connected app waits for the user's OK.

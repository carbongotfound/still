---
name: Spec from feedback
description: Groups feedback into themes and writes a spec.
when: Turning feedback or a request into a spec
---

1. Group the feedback into themes and count how often each comes up.
2. Pick the top theme and write a short spec: the problem, who has it, the change, and what's out of scope.
3. Add acceptance checks anyone can test, one per line.
4. Keep it under a page and list open questions at the end.

---
name: Smoke test
description: Clicks through your main pages and lists what breaks.
when: Testing a site or a release
---

1. Get the link from Product and stack, or ask for it once and save it there.
2. Open the main pages and try the main paths in a tab, like a careful new user.
3. List anything broken or confusing with steps to reproduce, what you expected and what happened.
4. Add new bugs to Known bugs, and mark fixed ones as fixed. Never submit forms that buy, send or delete.

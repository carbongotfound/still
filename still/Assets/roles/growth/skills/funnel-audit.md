---
name: Funnel audit
description: Walks your site like a newcomer and finds where people drop off.
when: Looking at the funnel, signups or a landing page
---

## When
The user wants to know why people don't sign up, don't activate, or leave a page.

## Inputs
- The site's address and the moment people get value, from Your product. If it's missing, ask once and save it.

## Steps
1. open_tab the site and go from first visit to signup to the first useful moment, like a first-time visitor.
2. At each step, note friction: slow loads, unclear copy, extra fields, dead ends, surprises.
3. Stop before any step that would buy, send or create a real account, and note what you couldn't see.
4. Rank the three biggest leaks by likely impact, each with its evidence and one fix.
5. Add the leaks to Your numbers so later tests can measure them.

## Check
- Each leak names the exact page and step where it happens.
- Each fix is small enough to test.

## Deliverable
The three leaks ranked, each with where, why it hurts, and the fix. Then the one to fix first.

## If it goes wrong
- A step needs a sign-in or payment: stop there, say so, and ask the user whether to continue together.
- The site won't load: say so and check whether it's the site or the connection.

## Limits
Never submit a form that buys, sends or signs up for real. Likely impact is a judgment: say it is.

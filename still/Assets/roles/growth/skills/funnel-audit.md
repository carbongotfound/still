---
name: Funnel audit
description: Walks your site like a newcomer and finds where people drop off.
when: Looking at the funnel, signups or a landing page
---

1. Open the site in a tab and go from first visit to signup to the first useful moment, like a first-time visitor.
2. Note every point of friction: slow steps, unclear copy, extra fields, dead ends.
3. Rank the three biggest leaks by likely impact, each with the evidence and one fix.
4. Add the leaks to the Your numbers knowledge file so later tests can measure them.

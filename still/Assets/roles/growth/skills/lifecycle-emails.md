---
name: Lifecycle emails
description: Onboarding and win-back emails that lead to the next step.
when: Writing onboarding, activation or win-back emails
---

1. Read Your product for the moment people get value.
2. Write a short sequence: each email has one goal, one link and a subject line under 50 characters.
3. Say when each one goes out and what it should change in the numbers.
4. Drafts only, in draft blocks. Nothing is sent or scheduled by you.

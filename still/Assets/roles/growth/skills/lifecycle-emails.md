## When
The user needs onboarding, activation or win-back emails.

## Inputs
- Your product, for the moment people get value and what blocks them on the way.
- Who the sequence is for, and the user's voice.

## Steps
1. Pick the one action each email should lead to, in the order people need to take them.
2. Write a short sequence, usually three to five emails: one goal, one link, a subject line under 50 characters.
3. Say when each one goes out, counted from signup or last visit.
4. Name the number each email should move.

## Check
- Each email has exactly one goal and one link.
- Subject lines are under 50 characters.

## Deliverable
The sequence as a table (when, subject, goal, metric), then each email in a ```draft block.

## If it goes wrong
- The value moment is unclear: ask the user what new users do first when it works.
- The product has no email list yet: say so and suggest starting with the first email only.

## Limits
Drafts only. Nothing is sent or scheduled by you, and no result is promised.

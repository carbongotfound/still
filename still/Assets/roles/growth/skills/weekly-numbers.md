## When
The user reviews the week's numbers, or it's the weekly numbers routine.

## Inputs
- This week's signups, activation and churn: from a sheet or connected app the user allows, or pasted by the user.
- Your numbers, for last week and the baselines.

## Steps
1. Get this week's figures. If you can't reach them, ask for them once.
2. Compare with last week and with the baselines.
3. Name the biggest change and two likely reasons, and say how sure you are.
4. Suggest one experiment that fits the biggest change.
5. Update Your numbers with the new figures.

## Check
- Percent changes are calculated from the real figures, and you recheck the math.
- Small numbers are flagged as too small to read much into.

## Deliverable
A small table (metric, this week, last week, change), the biggest change in one line with its likely reason, and one experiment.

## If it goes wrong
- A metric is missing: show the others and say which is missing.
- The numbers look broken (a sudden zero, a spike): flag tracking as a possible cause before anything else.

## Limits
Don't change anything live. A likely reason is a guess until a test shows it: say so.

## When
The user is announcing a launch, a feature or news.

## Inputs
- What's launching, who it's for and the one thing it changes for them. Read the launch page or notes when there are some; ask only for what's missing.
- Audience, Your voice and What landed.

## Steps
1. Write the one change in a sentence, from the reader's side.
2. Write three hooks with different angles: the problem, the result, the story.
3. Pick the strongest, using What landed, and write the full post under it with one call to action.
4. Check it against Your voice and every claim against the launch notes.

## Check
- One idea, one call to action.
- Every claim about the product is in the launch notes or on its page.

## Deliverable
The three hooks, then the full post in a ```draft block, then one line on which hook you'd pick and why.

## If it goes wrong
- You don't know what it changes for the reader: ask that one question before writing.
- The voice file is empty: write plainly and offer to learn the voice from two or three of their posts.

## Limits
Drafts only. You never post or schedule, and you never invent a customer, a quote or a number.

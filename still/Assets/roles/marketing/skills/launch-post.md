---
name: Launch post
description: Launch posts with three hooks to choose from.
when: Announcing a launch, a feature or news
---

1. Ask what's launching, who it's for and the one thing it changes for them.
2. Write three hooks with different angles: the problem, the result, the story.
3. Write the full post under the strongest hook, with a clear call to action.
4. Check What landed for what worked before, and say which hook you'd pick and why.

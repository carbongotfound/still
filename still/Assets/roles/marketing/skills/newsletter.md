## When
The user is writing a newsletter issue or an email to subscribers.

## Inputs
- What this issue is about and anything that must be in it. Ask only if neither the chat nor the open page says.
- Audience and Your voice.

## Steps
1. Pick the one main idea of the issue.
2. Write a subject line and three opening lines to choose from.
3. Write the body under 400 words, in the user's voice, with one call to action.
4. Read it through as a subscriber and cut what doesn't serve the main idea.

## Check
- Under 400 words, one main idea, one call to action.
- Links and facts come from the material the user gave or pages you read.

## Deliverable
The subject line, three openings, and the body in a ```draft block. Save it in Markdown with save_file if the user wants to keep it.

## If it goes wrong
- Too much must go in: suggest what to move to the next issue.
- The topic is thin: say so and suggest one angle that would make it worth sending.

## Limits
Drafts only. You never send or schedule the email.

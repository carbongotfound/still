---
name: Newsletter
description: A short issue with a subject line and opening options.
when: Writing a newsletter or an email to subscribers
---

1. Ask what this issue is about and anything that must be in it.
2. Write a subject line, three opening lines to choose from and a body under 400 words.
3. One main idea, one call to action, in the user's voice.
4. Drafts only. You never send or schedule the email.

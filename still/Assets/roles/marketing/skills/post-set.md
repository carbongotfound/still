name: One idea, every platform
description: Turns one idea into posts sized for each platform.
when: Turning an idea, launch or article into posts
---

## When
The user wants one idea, launch or article turned into posts for the places they post.

## Inputs
- The idea or the source (an article, a page, notes). Read it first.
- Audience, for who reads each platform, and the platforms the user posts on. Recall them; assume the two or three they mentioned before.

## Steps
1. Write the idea in one sentence.
2. For each platform, write a post sized and shaped for it, each with its own hook.
3. Add one image idea per post when it helps.
4. Check each post against Your voice.

## Check
- Each post fits its platform's usual length and format.
- No post makes a claim the source doesn't support.

## Deliverable
One ```draft block per platform, labelled with the platform, then which one you'd post first.

## If it goes wrong
- You don't know which platforms they use: write for two common ones and ask which to keep.
- The source is long: pick its single strongest point rather than summing it all up.

## Limits
Drafts only. Nothing is posted or scheduled by you.

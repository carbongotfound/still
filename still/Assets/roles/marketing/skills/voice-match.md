---
name: Voice match
description: Writes like you, from rules learned from your own posts.
when: Writing anything in the user's voice
---

1. Read the Your voice knowledge file. If it's empty, ask for two or three links to things the user wrote.
2. Sum up the voice in five short rules: sentence length, words they use and avoid, humor, structure, how they end.
3. Write with those rules, and check the draft against them before showing it.
4. When the user edits a draft, note what changed in Your voice.

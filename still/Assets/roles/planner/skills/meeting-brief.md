---
name: Meeting brief
description: Who's coming, what they do and what you want out of it.
when: Before a meeting or a call
---

1. Find the meeting on the calendar and read the invite and any thread about it.
2. Check People for what you already know about each guest, and look up the rest from public sources.
3. Write a two-minute brief: who's coming and their role, why the meeting exists, open points, and the outcome to aim for.
4. Save anything new about the guests to People.

name: Meeting brief
description: Who's coming, what they do and what you want out of it.
when: Before a meeting or a call
---

## When
The user has a meeting or call coming up and wants to walk in ready.

## Inputs
- The meeting: from a connected calendar, an invite on the page, or what the user tells you.
- People, for what you already know about each guest.

## Steps
1. Read the invite and any thread about it.
2. Check People for each guest, and look up the rest from public sources with web_search and read_url.
3. Find why the meeting exists and what's still open.
4. Write the brief, then save anything new about the guests to People.

## Check
- Each guest's role comes from a page you read, or is left out.
- The outcome to aim for is one sentence the user could say at the start.

## Deliverable
A two-minute brief: who's coming and their role, why the meeting exists, open points, and the outcome to aim for.

## If it goes wrong
- You can't see the calendar: ask which meeting, or work from the page they're on.
- A guest can't be found: say so instead of guessing who they are.

## Limits
Public information only about guests. Never accept, decline or move the meeting.

## When
The user wants a plan for tomorrow or the week, or it's the evening planning routine.

## Inputs
- Tomorrow's calendar and anything waiting in the inbox, from connected apps or pages the user allows.
- Priorities and Your week, for what matters and how they like to work.

## Steps
1. Read tomorrow's meetings and what's waiting on the user.
2. Pick the most important thing, using Priorities, and say why.
3. Fit the rest around the meetings, respecting focus hours in Your week.
4. Mark what could move or be skipped if the day runs over.

## Check
- Times are tomorrow's, with the day named, and nothing overlaps.
- Nothing on the list is already done.

## Deliverable
One list, readable in a minute: the most important thing first, then what fits around the meetings, then what could move.

## If it goes wrong
- You can't see the calendar or inbox: say so and plan from Priorities and what the user tells you.
- The day is overbooked: say what you'd move and why, instead of squeezing it in.

## Limits
Ask before accepting, declining or moving anything. Never invent a meeting.

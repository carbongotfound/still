---
name: Claim check
description: Checks whether a claim holds up, and how sure that is.
when: Checking a claim, a number or a quote
---

1. Find where the claim came from first.
2. Look for the primary source, then an independent one.
3. Say whether it holds up, partly holds up or doesn't, with how sure you are (high, medium or low) and why.
4. Give the accurate version the user could share instead.

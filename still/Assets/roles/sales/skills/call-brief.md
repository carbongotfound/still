---
name: Call brief
description: A one-page brief before every sales call.
when: Before a sales call or meeting with a prospect
---

## When
The user has a call or meeting coming up with a prospect or customer.

## Inputs
- The company, and the people on the call when known.
- Deals and Who buys, for what you already know, and What you sell, for where the offer fits.

## Steps
1. Recall and read Deals for any history with this company.
2. read_url their site, then web_search for news from the last six months and the public profiles of the people you'll likely meet.
3. Find the likely pain the user's offer solves, from what they do and what changed.
4. Write three sharp questions that test that pain, and the objections to expect.
5. Update Deals with anything new about the company.

## Check
- Every fact has a link to a public page you read.
- Names and titles are exact, or left out.

## Deliverable
One page: what they do in two lines, what changed lately (linked), who's in the room, where the offer fits, likely objections, three questions, and the one outcome to aim for.

## If it goes wrong
- You can't tell who's on the call: brief on the company and the roles that usually decide.
- Little is public: say so and keep the brief short instead of padding it.

## Limits
Public information only. Never guess a person's email, phone number or title, and never invent a reference customer.

---
name: Call brief
description: A one-page brief before every sales call.
when: Before a sales call or meeting with a prospect
---

1. Check the Deals and Who buys knowledge files for what you already know about this company.
2. Read their site, recent news and the people you'll likely meet, from public sources only.
3. Write one page: what they do, what changed lately, who's in the room, where your offer fits, likely objections and three sharp questions to ask.
4. End with the one outcome to aim for on the call.
Cite every fact with a link. If something can't be confirmed, leave it out.

---
name: Follow-up drafts
description: Short follow-ups in your voice, sent only on your OK.
when: Writing a follow-up, a first email or a reply to a prospect
---

1. Read what happened last with this person: the thread, the deal's next step, your notes.
2. Draft in the user's voice: under 90 words, one point, one clear ask with a date.
3. Offer two versions when the tone could go either way.
4. Put each draft in a draft block. Never send it yourself; ask first if the user wants it sent from a connected app.

---
name: Research brief
description: A one-page brief with a source for every claim.
when: Researching a topic or answering a hard question
---

1. Restate the question in one line and list the sub-questions.
2. Check Sources for sites to trust or avoid, then search wide and read the best sources.
3. Write the answer first, then key findings with a link each, caveats and sources with dates.
4. Save what's worth keeping to Findings.
